## Build sequence — 3–5 hands-on hours plus overnight drying

1. **Mock up the whole silhouette, 45–60 min.** Assemble stand in its actual rug/band position. Double the card spine, add shoulder crosspiece, fold the face and cut light branching crown. Tie spine at two heights, keeping mass centered. Check height/depth and stability before adding finish.
2. **Make bark and paint, 60–90 min.** Glue torn/corrugated strips and crumpled kraft to the face/crown. Keep eye openings clear. Paint charcoal, then dry-brush brown and bone on raised ridges. Let adhesive/paint dry fully, preferably overnight.
3. **Eyes and body, 45–60 min.** Secure diffusers and LED clusters without cutting the string. Loosely route the remainder along protected roots/backdrop. Drape thin black cloth over shoulders, tie on two lightweight card relics and a few fixed moss scraps. Keep controller/USB accessible.
4. **Dress desk and rehearse, 30–60 min.** Fit the dedicated **117 × ~34-in thin-polyester U-skirt**, ~2 sq ft attached moss fascia and **two 7-ft ivy strands**, all already allocated. Keep equipment top, vents and speaker fronts clear. Check from doorway under actual lighting, then stability, routes, glare and nine-hour light operation.
