## What changes

All **136 actual Grove Keeps and 886 Cuts** were read from canonical shared feedback. The previous batch overweighted a handful of recent retro/swing wins. Jamie Berry's Light up the Night / Dance Dance, Tape Five's Bad Boy Good Man / Geraldines Routine, and Parov Stelar's Catgroove / Clap Your Hands split within the same artists. This defeats artist/genre expansion as a sufficient selection method; it does not identify the cause of a Cut.

The broader evidence also contains **Great DJ / Hands**, **One Life Stand / Ready For The Floor and Night And Day**, **Ordinary Pleasure / Say That**, and **Ice Cream / Tight Fit** Keep-versus-Cut distinctions. Together with Bruises, I Belong In Your Arms, Daylight, Heart It Races, yes baby, make you mine, Joy, Kisses and Fasta, they support several distinct listening propositions rather than one uniform sound. These are editorial interpretations; the host's explicit brief and current balance correction have priority.

This test has **no deliberate swing/jazz selections**. It varies rhythmic indie, playful electro-pop, sensual electronic R&B and vocal dance. These descriptions are not quotas or claimed track-level acoustic measurements. Oh Land appears twice with seven intervening songs; every other credited artist appears once and every album once. Known catalog credits are not a complete shared-producer audit. Targeted discovery; historical saved-pool options were considered privately, without claiming current Like membership.

Specific hook, voice and rhythmic relationships lead each proposition. Published words are checked separately; pleasant lyrics, genre/BPM/energy and critical praise do not establish musical fit. No model, numerical fit gate, agent votes or listening scores.
