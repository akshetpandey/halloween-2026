## Buy / borrow / rent
**First choice:** borrow two LED wash fixtures and reuse existing lamps.  
**Purchase fallback:** two reputable compact RGB LED washes with independent static settings. Require accessible brightness, sound sensitivity and ability to disable strobe. Confirm model manual before buying.  
**Rental fallback:** request only two or four lights, not a venue package.

## What “works with the music” means
Sound-active fixtures listen through a microphone and react to sound. They are not guaranteed beat-grid synchronization. Start with one static red wash and one gentle sound-responsive fixture; if it looks chaotic, use slow automatic fades or fully static light.

A synchronized DMX/software show is an optional later project. It requires compatible hardware, setup and rehearsal, and is not necessary for this party.

## Practical installation
- Use floor/wall bounce to create shadows while keeping fixtures protected at the perimeter.
- Keep hot surfaces and equipment clear of fabric/paper according to the manufacturer.
- Use indoor-rated equipment where appropriate; keep liquids away from plugs and supplies.
- Record outlet/circuit locations; add actual labeled wattages of equipment before connecting.
- Do not daisy-chain power strips or conceal cables under a loose rug.
- No lasers or rapid strobes in the default design. No fog/haze required to make this work.
- Keep smoke detectors, sprinklers and vents unobstructed.
- Photograph the final settings and tape a simple “restore scene” instruction near controls.
