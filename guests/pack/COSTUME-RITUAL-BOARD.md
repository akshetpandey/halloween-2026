# The antlered court: ritual costume inspiration

The Yellowjackets reference sounds like the **Antler Queen**, the veiled figure with an antler headdress. The exact scene is not confirmed, so this collection also includes **Lottie’s Doomcoming crown**. The captions here focus on costume design and avoid plot details.

This direction adds ceremonial silhouettes to the party’s enchanted woodland palette: branching crowns, open-knit veils, weathered ivory, tarnished gold, moss and oxblood. These are references for inventing your own forest entity; anyone can wear any look.

## An easy Antler Queen-inspired outfit

1. **Base:** an oversized ivory knit, robe, tunic or cream layers you already own.
2. **Crown:** compact, lightweight faux antlers on a broad headband; keep tips soft.
3. **Veil:** a short open-knit scarf or lace offcut, loose enough to lift easily. Wear it behind or beside the face for dancing.
4. **Finish:** a cord belt, one dark-red ribbon and a small handmade charm. Add a weathered-looking shawl if you want more volume.

For the open-face Doomcoming version, use the crown and one trailing ribbon without the veil. For a darker club look, keep the same silhouette in charcoal and bone.

Suggested personal DIY spending cap: **$25–$50 if reusing base clothes**. This is a planning target, not a researched shopping quote or a charge against the host’s decor budget. Start with the crown and borrowed fabric before buying a full costume.

## The eight references and how to borrow from them

### The veiled antler oracle

![Antler Queen — Yellowjackets](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/antler-queen.jpg)

**Antler Queen — Yellowjackets**

Use an oversized ivory knit or thrifted robe as the base. Add a short open-knit veil, a cord belt and soft antlers. Arrange the veil behind or beside your face while dancing. The sculptural outline does most of the work.

Credit: Costume design: Marie Schley. Photograph: Kailey Schwerman / Paramount+ with SHOWTIME. Production publicity image.

[Image source and context](https://theartofcostume.com/2025/06/11/rewriting-the-rules-of-survival-fashion-marie-schley-on-costumes-in-yellowjackets-season-3/)

### The woodland celebrant

![Lottie’s Doomcoming crown — Yellowjackets](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/doomcoming.jpg)

**Lottie’s Doomcoming crown — Yellowjackets**

Keep your face open: a small crown, muted dress or shirt and trousers, and one ribbon trailing down your back. This is the easiest interpretation of the Yellowjackets reference for a party.

Credit: Yellowjackets production still reproduced by Autostraddle. Individual still photographer not identified on the source.

[Image source and context](https://www.autostraddle.com/how-to-diy-a-yellowjackets-halloween-costume/)

### The lace forest sovereign

![Dress, Widows of Culloden, autumn/winter 2006–07 — Alexander McQueen](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/mcqueen-antlers.jpg)

**Dress, Widows of Culloden, autumn/winter 2006–07 — Alexander McQueen**

An ivory slip, shirt or tunic with a secondhand lace shawl is enough. Use a small antler headband to lift one corner of the fabric. Borrow the contrast of delicate lace and wild branches; the gown and train are optional.

Credit: Alexander McQueen; cream silk tulle and lace with resin antlers. Photograph © Sølve Sundsbø / Art + Commerce, as credited by The Met. Image copy sourced from HuffPost image CDN.

[Image source and context](https://blog.metmuseum.org/alexandermcqueen/dress-widows-of-culloden/)

### The dusk-crowned monarch

![King Arthur’s halo crown — The Green Knight (2021)](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/arthur-halo.jpg)

**King Arthur’s halo crown — The Green Knight (2021)**

Cut a small lightweight halo from craft foam, paint it tarnished gold and secure it to a broad headband. A dark shawl with a few metallic fabric patches suggests ceremonial robes. No metal fabrication needed.

Credit: The Green Knight film still via Natania Barron. Costume design: Malgosia Turzanska; crown development with David Samuel Houghton. Individual still photographer not confirmed.

[Image source and context](https://nataniabarron.com/2021/08/03/knight-in-gold-velvet-costume-in-the-green-knight/) · [Additional film/design context](https://observer.com/2021/08/the-green-knight-costume-designer-interview/)

### The bark guardian

![The Green Knight — The Green Knight (2021)](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/green-knight.jpg)

**The Green Knight — The Green Knight (2021)**

Wear brown or charcoal layers with a leaf-covered shoulder or collar. Draw a few branching lines with skin-safe makeup on one cheek. Skip the armor; the texture and asymmetry communicate the creature.

Credit: The Green Knight film still via Natania Barron. Costume design: Malgosia Turzanska; creature prosthetics are a separate effects contribution. Individual still photographer not confirmed.

[Image source and context](https://nataniabarron.com/2021/08/03/knight-in-gold-velvet-costume-in-the-green-knight/) · [Additional film/design context](https://observer.com/2021/08/the-green-knight-costume-designer-interview/)

### The old forest faun

![Faun makeup reference — Pan’s Labyrinth (2006)](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/faun.jpg)

**Faun makeup reference — Pan’s Labyrinth (2006)**

Small curled horns with a brown vest or open shirt and trousers work without a full creature suit. Use forehead spirals or textured cuffs as the extra detail. This photo shows professional makeup: borrow the shapes rather than the whole build.

Credit: Production makeup photograph reproduced by Monster Legacy. Faun effects: DDT; performer Doug Jones. Individual photographer not identified.

[Image source and context](https://monsterlegacy.net/2014/12/22/monster-gallery-pans-labyrinth-2006/)

### The harvest apparition

![Strohmann — Wilder Mann (2010–2011), Charles Fréger](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/strohmann.jpg)

**Strohmann — Wilder Mann (2010–2011), Charles Fréger**

Use cream fabric strips as a short shoulder fringe over a simple outfit, then add one oxblood ribbon. Keep the silhouette narrow enough for the apartment. This is a real folk-costume photography reference, not a claim that the pictured tradition is Samhain.

Credit: Photograph © Charles Fréger, Wilder Mann, 2010–2011. Image identified as Strohmann in the photographer’s archive filename.

[Image source and context](https://www.charlesfreger.com/portfolio/wilder-mann-fr/)

### The autumn flower deity

![May Queen floral costume — Midsommar (2019)](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/guests/inspiration/ritual/may-queen.jpg)

**May Queen floral costume — Midsommar (2019)**

Our autumn interpretation: cluster a few fabric flowers and leaves on one shoulder or a headband. Repeat the colors in your clothes. The inspiration is floral abundance; you do not need a full flower-covered dress.

Credit: Midsommar production image reproduced by The Independent; individual still photographer not confirmed.

[Image source and context](https://www.independent.co.uk/life-style/fashion/florence-pugh-midsommar-flower-dress-sell-auction-price-a24-a9523386.html) · [Additional film/design context](https://a24films.com/films/midsommar)

## Choosing for this party

- **Closest to the requested reference:** Antler Queen and Doomcoming.
- **Beautiful and eerie:** McQueen lace with small antlers.
- **More ceremonial:** a tarnished halo and dark cloak.
- **More creature-like:** bark guardian or faun.
- **Harvest rather than horror:** fabric fringe or an autumn flower collar.

Keep headpieces compact, hems off the floor and shoes comfortable. Use fabric strips rather than loose straw for a fringe costume. One strong detail over an outfit you already own will read well in the room lighting.
