Either 24-case is 500 sq ft nominal, so it cannot reproduce the ~609-sq-ft gathered-wall target even before cut waste, headers or furniture. Each small throw is too short for a wall drop: pairing two 60-in heights with a 2-in horizontal overlap gives **118 in**, requiring a real stitched or tested mechanically joined seam; tape adhesion to fleece is not assumed. At most twelve nominal 50-in-wide pairs, then vertical overlaps and folds consume width. Cheap nominal area therefore brings more joining work, extra fasteners and uncertain opacity; existing 72 clips are not automatically a complete joined-throw mounting kit.

**Value recommendation:** for a cloth target around $100–130, consider fewer oversized blankets and flatter/selective coverage, preserving the chosen plush material and simple full-height hanging. Full enclosure with the same gathering and furnishing coverage remains the nine-piece proposal. No extra acoustic performance is assumed, and no alternative is silently ordered or substituted.
