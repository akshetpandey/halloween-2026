## Recommended silhouette
**City of Gods exception, confirmed October 21:** smaller bone-look crown rather than large antlers for the crowded external event. [Latest weekend direction](05-weekend-costumes-2026-10-21.md#latest-direction) and [illustrative renders](06-weekend-costume-renders-2026-10-21.md). This does not settle or cancel the larger October 31 reference target below.

**Weathered ivory king:** owned dark-green trousers and shoes with an oversized cream tunic/shirt; a short cape or asymmetrical shoulder mantle; natural-brown upright forked antlers matching the [Yellowjackets reference](references/README.md); a removable full-face veil using the preferred gold metallic netting direction; a dark belt and one oxblood detail. The trousers remain a reuse candidate rather than a tested full outfit. See [current decisions and sourcing](03-sourcing-shortlist.md). The fast weekend headpieces are acknowledged aesthetic compromises, not a final screen-accurate crown decision.

The reference's key relationship is two upright, relatively narrow antler clusters framing a tall hanging mesh veil. Broad white stag fans, palmate elk antlers and ornate forehead crowns are not substitutes the host has accepted. This is a visual reading of the saved photograph, not a verified species identification or dimensional blueprint. The costume designer describes the original veil as a crocheted 1990s dress; actual metal chainmail would be an intentional reinterpretation.

The crown and mantle are the two useful places to spend. Avoid padding the outfit with many accessories that disappear under low room light.

| Mode | Appearance | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Host | Antlers, mantle, face clear; veil clipped back | Greeting, drinks, moving around |
| Oracle king | Veil lowered briefly, hands free | Photo or 60–90-second story moment |
| Dance | Veil off; mantle removed if warm | Comfortable movement; crown stays only if secure |

## Build sequence
1. Fit the base outfit first; do a squat/sit/stairs test and check pockets.
2. Choose a broad headband or fitted lightweight cap base for the selected replicas. Soft foam remains a comfort option, but it must have the right geometry. A resin/printed candidate requires actual weight and mounting checks; do not assume a seller's lightweight claim proves all-night comfort. Do not substitute real antlers or heavy resin without a separate fit decision.
3. Shape the silhouette upward rather than far sideways. Final width depends on doors and the host’s comfort, so do not order oversized antlers without measurements.
4. Trial the gold netting as a tall drape using a padded, lightweight support with accessible clips. Do not load sharp antler tips or stretch fabric tightly across the face. A plain brow-hung panel is a different silhouette from the reference's elevated canopy; finalize the support only after the antler geometry is chosen. Keep eyes/face clear for walking, serving and dancing; check snagging, dim-light visibility and independent removal. The previous metal-veil support proposal is superseded; mail is no longer the preferred material.
5. Add a short shoulder mantle and cord belt. Secure loose fabric and make the mantle removable quickly.
6. Add one small bone-like charm and one oxblood detail. The three raven skulls already in the decor cart have scenery allocations; do not double-count them as costume supplies without reassigning one.
7. Rehearse 30–45 minutes while talking, sitting and moving. Test with the actual room lighting before finalizing.

## Alternative palettes
- **Ivory and bone:** closest to Antler Queen; strong against the dark bedroom.
- **Charcoal and old gold:** a darker ceremonial king; use the Green Knight halo mood as a secondary reference, not a second heavy crown.
- **Ivory with moss/ochre:** softer woodland ruler; bring autumn in through one shoulder ornament.
