## Selection and variety

**40 new lead artists and 10 winner follow-ups.** Returning leads are Gorillaz, Gorgon City, Scissor Sisters, Vitalic, Dragonette, Jessie Ware, Duke Dumont, Goldfrapp, SG Lewis and Daft Punk. Each contributes one recording. No Spotify URI or ISRC repeats any of the previous 191; each album ID appears once within this batch. The existing party and earlier audition playlists are unchanged.

The order alternates follow-ups with four new leads rather than grouping albums or genres. New directions include short, assertive club-pop (Charli xcx, Cobrah, Tove Lo), sensual bass-pop (Shygirl, AlunaGeorge), French touch/disco (Modjo, Stardust, Cassius, Spiller), melodic alternative dance (Cut Copy, Friendly Fires, LCD Soundsystem) and darker classic synth-pop (Depeche Mode, The Knife, Pet Shop Boys). These are editorial lanes, separate from Spotify artist genres.

Lead-artist counts are not independent-performer counts: Fever Ray and The Knife share Karin Dreijer; Sophie Ellis-Bextor appears solo and with Spiller; Thomas Bangalter appears in Daft Punk and Stardust. These connections are separated in the sequence. Goldfrapp now has three recordings in the pool, and Purple Disco Machine/Kylie/Confidence Man receive no further expansion here. Final programming still needs collaboration/alias-aware concentration limits.

Radio/short versions are selected where available, including Hung Up, Emerge, Personal Jesus, Tribulations, Over And Over, I Got U and Music Sounds Better With You. Only Lady (5:07) and Digital Love (5:01) exceed five minutes. Their later passages deserve scrutiny. Some shorter hooks are intentionally repetitive: Stupidisco, Over And Over, Silver Screen and GOOD PUSS are tests of whether the sound earns the repetition. Santigold and AlunaGeorge provide rhythmic/intensity contrast; they should be cut if they reduce momentum too much. No intros/outros/interludes by title; full-track listening still determines dead space and suitability.
