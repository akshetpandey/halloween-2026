## Identity and appearance brief

The physical guardian is a **gold branch floor lamp**, not a leafy tree figurine. Preserve the tall thin tapering gold trunk, irregular alternating twig stubs, asymmetric Y fork, broad tapered linen shade, and splayed four/five-point root base. Use antique honey gold, lighter gold ridges, bronze recesses, parchment fabric and a warm inner lamp glow. The photos are strongly amber-lit; do not copy that as an orange plastic surface. No photo pixels, room/background geometry or scan surfaces belong in the public model.

Only the upper trunk knot broadens for small glossy eyes and carved brows/smile below the brim. The shade is its dignified hat. Keep the base rooted, the lower trunk narrow and broad negative spaces between twigs. A luminous magical ledger leaf is an acting prop, distinct from the real lamp silhouette. No separate round mascot head, legs, fox circle or hopping lamp base.
