# Food, water and house supplies
Status: Quantity framework ready. Owner: hospitality helper TBD. Working budget $180 food + $90 service/cleanup supplies.

## Food direction
A casual late-night grazing table fits the party: bread/crackers, cheese, grapes/apples, olives/pickles, a substantial vegetarian dip, pretzels and a sweet autumn bite. Use ordinary serving trays with themed labels. Food stays separate from decorative moss, wood and costume-making materials.

At 9:30 p.m., this can be explicitly a drinks-and-snacks party, but a 4 a.m. finish warrants a substantial refill. Reserve money for pizza or another easy late-night option rather than elaborate individual canapés.

## Draft 45-person shopping quantities
These are starting estimates, not a catering guarantee; adjust for actual attendance and diets.
- 3–4 lb cheese or equivalent savory protein options.
- 3–4 lb substantial dips/spreads, with one clearly vegan option.
- 4–5 lb grapes/apples and cut vegetables.
- 3–4 lb crackers, bread, pretzels and chips.
- About 54 small sweet pieces for 45 guests, or 72 for 60 guests, if choosing the proposed one-per-guest [Moon Well chocolate offering](bathroom-installation/README.md). This replaces the prior 45–60-piece sweet allowance; it is not an additional candy order. Reserve some at the living-room food table and adjust for diets and actual packaged counts.
- One later substantial refill; choose pizzas or sandwiches based on diets and vendor quote.
- Water: easy refill station throughout; start with 24–36 L available/refillable for 45 guests, then monitor. Apartment tap water plus clean dispensers can be far cheaper than individual bottles.
- Seltzer/NA drinks: roughly 1–2 packaged servings per guest who prefers them; buy only after considering the NA punch plan.

The $180 food line is a target for homemade/retail snacks and a modest refill. At 60 guests or with substantial catered food, raise it or reallocate from scenery.

## Service quantities
For 45 attendees: roughly 120–150 cups if guests reuse named cups, 60–90 small plates, 150–200 napkins, 2–3 serving tongs/spoons, bin liners and paper towels. Scale with guest count and actual menu. Mark cups to reduce waste.

## House checklist
- [ ] Clear coat/bag storage that does not use the dance floor or hallway.
- [ ] Hide valuables and private documents before guests arrive.
- [ ] Fresh hand soap, plenty of toilet paper and backup hand towels.
- [ ] Bathroom bin with spare liner.
- [ ] Visible water station and clearly marked trash/recycling.
- [ ] Spill kit near kitchen: towels, absorbent cloth and cleaner suitable for the floor.
- [ ] Fridge and cooler plan for all perishable food/drink.
- [ ] Ingredients/allergens listed; separate utensils for different options.
- [ ] Small first-aid kit and accessible normal lighting.
- [ ] One helper checks water, bathroom and bins every 45–60 minutes.

Completion: enough storage, serving surfaces and replenishment capacity for the actual guest flow, not merely enough purchased food.
