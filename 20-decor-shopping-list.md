## Curtain calculation before ordering
The linked pair supplies **104 in of total flat fabric width**. At the proposed 1.5× fullness it covers about **69 in / 5.8 ft of rod span**. Calculate each separate window/backdrop run as `ceil(1.5 × span in inches ÷ 104)` pairs; do not pool leftovers across disconnected windows without a layout. Measure rod-to-finish height separately; 84 in is only the researched example length. Prevent fabric pooling in walking/dancing areas.
