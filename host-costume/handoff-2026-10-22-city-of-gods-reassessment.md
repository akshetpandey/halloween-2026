## Evidence checked and design reading

Adjustable layers remain practical for the indoor/outdoor event.
- Visually inspected the official costume page in Chrome. The first inspiration row includes radiating solar headpieces and metallic face adornment, gold-painted/metallic botanical styling, and a dark skeletal crowned figure with extensive neck/chest ornament. These are curated inspiration images, **not evidence of what every attendee wears or a single mandatory palette**. The page's footer includes older copyright text; do not call every reference a new 2026 costume.
- **Styling assessment, not an event rule:** the existing Eclipse has a clear otherworldly symbol plus metallic body ornament and dark contrast. Autumn Court's oxblood poet shirt/plain ivory sash reads more Renaissance/fantasy noble than spectacular deity. A harvest/fae sovereign belongs in the broad brief, but the existing render lacks the transformation/adornment the host wants. It is not the current assistant front-runner.
- Wild Hunt's fur, green trousers and pale branching crown are a useful base; its lace-up ivory shirt is the main Renaissance cue. Recommended development is a **Gilded Stag / woodland deity**, an invented styling concept rather than a historically accurate named-god portrayal.

## Proposed development — unselected and unpriced

Keep the compact pale bone-look crown, green trousers and black fur. Replace the lace-up poet-shirt styling with a clean fitted black open-neck or mesh base. Concentrate the ornament around a broad antique-gold leaf/branch collar or collarbone/chest piece, plus cuffs and a small branching metallic face accent. Avoid simply repeating Friday's delicate full-body chain. No tall antlers, weapons, full-face veil, floor-length fabric or new coat required by this proposal.

Intended contrast: Friday celestial black/bright gold with solar rays and chains; Saturday dark forest green/bone/antique gold with organic forms. Fur can be reused, but its receipt, fit and warmth remain unverified. A collar must remain comfortable, lightweight and quickly removable; it is not automatically an expensive rigid metal neckpiece. New collar, makeup/base and cuff choices require sourcing and live availability checks before being added to the shopping list. No new render or exact products selected this turn.

Autumn Court could instead be substantially restyled as an opulent harvest deity with metallic botanical ornament and a decorated drape, but that is a new design direction, not evidence the previous render already meets the host's aesthetic target.
