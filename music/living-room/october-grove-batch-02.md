Prepared September 8, 2026. **25 new candidates, 25 distinct lead artists, 1:33:14 raw.** Live [Spotify batch](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/3mlcsJdQawKVlWVyfEPA72) has all 25 exact URIs in the prepared order, verified through the API. The host finished all 141 prior tracks and kept four: Now U Do, Hypnotized, Chemicals and Joy. The other 137 are explicit cuts, with no written reasons. All original votes are preserved in the private database; no rejected recording or alternate release with the same ISRC is recycled here. Feedback remains local and is not copied into the seed file.

The latest instruction is **much more variation and much higher energy, in batches of about 25**. The previous concentration on restrained house and atmospheric instrumentals did not fit. Working inference: prominent hooks, vocal character, physical bounce and production impact matter more than a tight BPM window. The four keeps span roughly 108–137 catalog BPM and 0.812–0.971 catalog energy. Those figures describe the keepers; they do not establish an automatic acceptance rule.

Five lanes, five tracks each, are interleaved: vocal dance-pop, funk/disco, electro/percussion, UK bounce/garage and darker/breakbeat probes. Four tracks follow the existing winners' artists/collaborators; most introduce new lead artists. Robyn appears once solo and once as an Impact collaborator. No lead artist repeats and no album contributes a second track. Short edits reduce long club openings; none of the selected titles is an intro, outro or interlude. No one has independently listened through all of these versions for this task, so dead space, repetition and suitability still need the host's vote.

Parcels is a lighter live-band contrast; The Prodigy is an upper aggression test; Rudimental tests drum-and-bass rhythm; Goldfrapp and RÜFÜS DU SOL test darker/sensual texture. These are intentional boundary checks rather than claims that every track should fill the living room. Some familiar hooks repeat; their ability to hold attention is part of this audition. The nine-hour requirement applies to the eventual approved programme, not this batch.
