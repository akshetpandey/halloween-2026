# Fewer fleece hooks — October 29, 2026

**Recommendation correction:** six hooks per blanket was a conservative spacing choice, not a weight requirement. Start with **three points across each 90-in blanket top** (left, middle, right); add a fourth where sag, a corner or edge coverage needs it. That gives approximately 45-in spacing with three,30-in with four. The result may have gentle swags rather than a taut straight top. This is a practical layout recommendation, not a manufacturer-certified blanket mounting system.

**Buy two [14-hook Command Large packs](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075Y2M1Q5), 28 hooks total**, instead of three 42-hook packs. Six blankets need 18–24 points, leaving 4–10 spares. ## What was checked

- The purchased [BEDELITE Black King 108 × 90 listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08FYBWRQ1), Measurements panel, shows **Item Weight 3.66 pounds**, checked directly in Chrome October 29. This is a listing specification, not the received blanket weighed by host; the earlier 6.29-lb value belonged to the larger 120-in square and does not describe the purchased King.
- The selected large hooks are the stated 5-lb model. More hanging points help spread and shape the fabric; do not multiply hook ratings into a new capacity for one item. [3 M’s own multi-hook project guidance](https://www.command.com.my/3M/en_MY/command-my/projects/all-projects/his-office-efficient-organization/) keeps the total rating at one hook’s rating and calls for even distribution.
- [3 M’s US FAQ](https://www.command.com/3M/en_US/command/contact-us/faqs/) calls for the correct surface, clean/dry preparation and package wait time; many hooks need at least one hour. Extra strips do not automatically raise capacity. Follow the actual pack instructions.

The whole listed 3.66-lb blanket is below the stated 5-lb single-hook rating, which is why weight alone does not justify six. One top point would bunch the blanket, so several are still useful for a broad backdrop. Actual clip grip, folded hem, wall finish and sag need a one-panel trial after receipt; no load/adhesion test passed here. If the actual blanket plus attachment exceeds the rating or the substrate is unsuitable, more hooks are not the fix—change the support method.

The **96 binder-clip pack stays unchanged**:18–24 wall clips + 6 desk = 24–30 used,66–72 spare. Six fleece/four moss remain host-reported ordered; other purchases unconfirmed. No extra blanket or acoustic backing introduced; lighting remains deferred.
