# Purple October Grove + glowing moon
Updated September 3, 2026. Confirmed host refinement: Oasis is acceptable provided it gets very dim; living room should have a glowing moon plus dim purple light across the room. This supersedes the previous mostly warm living-room color recommendation. No lighting purchased, moved, configured or tested.

## Proposed scene
A steady, low violet/lavender wash across pale walls and ceiling creates the overall forest-night atmosphere. Add a neutral-white glowing moon as the focal point and a few very low warm accents around drinks/seating. Keep the living room calm; music-reactive club effects remain in the bedroom.

Start with two indirect RGB sources on different sides of the room, aimed at wall/ceiling surfaces rather than guests. This is a layout proposal pending photos, not a guarantee that two fixtures cover the space. Test one source first and judge spill/coverage before buying its pair. Windows will not reflect like pale walls; curtains and wall color matter. Do not assume a small moon lamp can also wash the room purple.

## Oasis: new verified specifications and remaining gate
Source: [Single Ambient](https://heyoasis.com/en/shop/products/ambient-light-us). Public page data accessed September 3; extracted specs saved in research/oasis-specs-2026-09-03.json.
- Tone range: **2000–4000 K**. This documents warm-to-neutral whites, not RGB purple. Plan a separate color source unless Oasis supplies contrary specifications for a selected different model.
- Brightness: **500 lumens** nominal; power **7 W**; listed cable **6.5 ft**.
- Dimmable: yes. Page data also advertises smooth full-range dimming, but provides **no numeric minimum output or independently verified low-brightness performance**. Do not translate “dimmable” into a promise of candle-level brightness or use nominal 500 lm to calculate an unsupported minimum.
- Page lists $45 per Ambient. [Standard Room Kit](https://heyoasis.com/en/shop/products/standard-room-kit) shows $190 with four Ambients and two bulbs; selected variants/terms and delivery need confirmation.
- Manufacturer FAQ says dim in the app rather than a mains dimmer. General manufacturer dimming claims are not a hands-on flicker test.

Oasis is now the host-accepted conditional choice for warm ambient/daily lighting. Do not keep pushing a different entire ecosystem solely because earlier research preferred it. Trial one unit at its lowest setting in the intended room before committing a large pack. Acceptance: comfortably low output at seated eye level, usable adjustment at the bottom, no distracting observed flicker/buzzing, and no adaptive routine overriding the party scene. Dimming depth remains unresolved until evidence or a trial, not a reason to declare Oasis unsuitable.

## Glowing moon
Prefer an actual illuminated lunar disk on the wall or a sufficiently large moon globe, set steady neutral white and dimmer than the TV. Aim for roughly 12–16 inches if it should read as a room focal point; 6-inch table globes are small accents. Final size/location depends on photos and mounting.

Amazon lead: [Skylumate / QIYIMEILUX 9.5-inch plug-in dimmable moon wall sconce, B0B41JXKSP](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B41JXKSP).
