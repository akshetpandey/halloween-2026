## Recommended build
Use 12 spirits as a planning midpoint, with the final count anywhere from 10–15. Each has one nearby moon touchpoint and one unique website URL. A moon is the universal instruction “tap here,” not a distinct score type. Give the spirits individual names/lore. Guest progress uses the actual number of active spirits; do not hard-code ten.

The spirit can be a lightweight bark face/relief attached to scenery. Suggested assembly: cardboard backing, layered torn cork or textured paper for bark, small air-dry-clay facial features, dark brown/charcoal paint and a pale dry-brush. Change brows, nose, twig horns and a small identifying detail to make each face distinct. Keep the consistent finish; no need for fifteen intricate sculptures. Make one sample to establish craft time before batch-making.

For a separate touchpoint, stack from the guest-facing side:
1. Pale glow moon, or glow circle with a dark crescent/detail.
2. Thin opaque dark paper/vinyl circle about 35–40 mm wide when needed to conceal the whole tag.
3. Complete 25–30 mm NFC sticker tag, centered behind the visible target.
4. A removable mounting carrier suited to the surface, or a thin scenic panel secured to the decor.

A narrow die-cut crescent alone will not hide a round tag. A full-moon disk can cover it directly if sufficiently large and opaque. Cut ONLY the decorative covering, never the NFC antenna. Put the antenna behind the actual moon target, not off to one side. Use nonmetal layers; test the exact pigment/adhesive and finished assembly. A metal shelf or window frame can still interfere even though the decorative top is nonmetal. Keep separate from metal or use a tested on-metal tag.

Do not assume the permanent adhesive on an NFC sticker becomes removable because its decorative top is removable. Prefer a replaceable carrier on scenery; test adhesion/removal on the actual surface before putting tags on painted walls. Rough bark is a poor sticker surface; keep the touchpoint flat and smooth. Mount it immediately below/beside its spirit, within the same visual grouping, with room for the top edge of an iPhone to approach.

## Glow and visibility
Treat glow as a bonus. Make a pale moon contrast with its dark backing under the real amber/dusk lighting, even after its glow fades. The spirit can be partially concealed; once discovered its moon should be easy to reach. Avoid identical moon touchpoint decals elsewhere without tags, which would create false targets.

Cricut recommends at least 30 minutes of direct-light charging for its removable glow vinyl and describes approximately 30 minutes of glow before recharge. That is product-specific, not a promise for all glow stickers. Do not plan all-night glow from one charge, or assume dim warm lights maintain it. Test visibility at setup, after an hour and later in the evening. No extra UV lighting is required for this design. [Cricut glow FAQ](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/7054720637719-Cricut-Vinyl)
