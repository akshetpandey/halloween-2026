# The Hollow playback

Play `The-Hollow-party.mp3` once at original speed (1.0x) through the output router after hardware verification. Rekordbox and the FLX4 are optional. Keep shuffle, repeat, Auto DJ/Automix, automatic normalization and extra DJ transition effects off. The musical transitions, tempo changes and mix EQ are already rendered. Speaker-specific EQ, gain and alignment delay belong downstream in the output buses and are still appropriate. The single-item `The-Hollow-play.m3u8` selects only the party file, which ends after the finale; reserve does not start automatically.

The audio-output session owns the hardware route: a small playback-only REAPER project plus an Aggregate Device is the stereo baseline, with separate Audioengine USB L/R and optical soundbar L/R buses. Encoded optical 5.1 requires a separate AC-3/passthrough backend and coordinated PCM-plus-bitstream transport; a plain Aggregate Device does not establish that. Apply soundbar channel EQ/delay before encoding, keep the encoded carrier intact, and calibrate the earlier speaker feed against the later feed. Hardware passthrough, channel mapping and long-run synchronization remain untested. See `music/mac-orchestration.md` and `music/optical-5-1-pipeline-2026-10-29.md`.

The lossless `The-Hollow-continuous.flac` can feed downstream DSP or a calibrated 5.1 derivative without rebuilding the musical mix. It includes reserve: constrain normal party playback to 0–27330.109531 seconds (7:35:30.110), or use the already bounded party MP3. If using rekordbox as an optional player, use one deck at 0% pitch with beat/key Sync off.
