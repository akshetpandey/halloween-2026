# Cocktails and bar service
Status: Two original test recipes ready. Owner: bar helper TBD.
Working allocation: $420 including alcohol, mixers, nonalcoholic drinks and ice. Food and service supplies are separate.

## Design
Two deep-red drinks with distinct flavor profiles. Use the same service size and simple garnishes. Both recipes below are original starting formulas, not tested crowd recipes; taste one serving before scaling. Juices vary in sweetness and color.

All measures are US fluid ounces. Each alcoholic serving has 1.5 fl oz of 40% spirit: 0.6 fl oz pure alcohol. At the 7.5 fl oz recipe volume, that is about 8% ABV before extra ice melt. Label the actual spirit strength and recipe if changed.

## Blood of the Orchard
Bourbon, cranberry, apple, lemon and ginger. Bright garnet with warm harvest flavor.

| Ingredient | One serving | 20 servings |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bourbon, 40% ABV | 1.5 oz | 30 oz / 887 mL |
| Cranberry juice cocktail, sweetened | 2 oz | 40 oz / 1.183 L |
| Pasteurized apple cider | 1.5 oz | 30 oz / 887 mL |
| Fresh lemon juice | 0.5 oz | 10 oz / 296 mL |
| Chilled water | 0.5 oz | 10 oz / 296 mL |
| Nonalcoholic ginger beer, added last | 1.5 oz | 30 oz / 887 mL |
| **Total** | **7.5 oz** | **150 oz / 4.44 L** |

Chill all ingredients. Combine everything except ginger beer; add ginger beer just before service. Serve over ice. Garnish with a thin apple slice or expressed orange peel. Cranberry should dominate the color; cloudy cider may make it more rust-red.

Taste adjustment: if too sweet, test another 0.25 oz lemon per serving; if too sharp, test 0.25 oz simple syrup. Record the revised yield and batch numbers rather than improvising in the full bowl.

**Alcohol-free twin:** replace the 1.5 oz bourbon with 1 oz strong chilled black tea and 0.5 oz water. Use decaf if desired; label caffeine if present. Keep the other ingredients the same.

## The Blackthorn Pact
Gin, tart cherry, cranberry, lemon and rosemary. A forest-herb sour with a dark ruby color. “Blackthorn” is a fantasy name; the recipe contains no sloe/blackthorn fruit.

| Ingredient | One serving | 20 servings |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gin, 40% ABV | 1.5 oz | 30 oz / 887 mL |
| Unsweetened tart cherry juice | 2 oz | 40 oz / 1.183 L |
| Cranberry juice cocktail, sweetened | 1 oz | 20 oz / 591 mL |
| Fresh lemon juice | 0.5 oz | 10 oz / 296 mL |
| Rosemary simple syrup | 0.5 oz | 10 oz / 296 mL |
| Chilled water | 1 oz | 20 oz / 591 mL |
| Club soda, added last | 1 oz | 20 oz / 591 mL |
| **Total** | **7.5 oz** | **150 oz / 4.44 L** |

For the syrup, dissolve equal weights sugar and water with a few clean culinary rosemary sprigs; steep briefly off heat, taste, strain and cool. Start with a mild infusion. Measure finished syrup for the recipe. Keep the garnish short and out of guests’ faces.

**Alcohol-free twin:** replace gin with 1 oz chilled rosemary tea and 0.5 oz water. Everything else stays the same. No bitters or alcoholic substitute needed.

## Scaling assumptions
For initial procurement, plan **2 alcoholic cocktail servings per total guest** as a planning allowance, not a consumption goal. Adjust for confirmed non-drinkers, duration, BYOB and other alcohol. Do not add a full beer/wine allowance on top without reducing punch quantities.

Equal split between recipes:
| Total guests | Total cocktail servings | Each recipe | Total liquid | Bourbon needed | Gin needed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 60 | 30 | 13.31 L | 1.33 L | 1.33 L |
| 45 | 90 | 45 | 19.96 L | 2.00 L | 2.00 L |
| 60 | 120 | 60 | 26.62 L | 2.66 L | 2.66 L |

Bottle check: two 750 mL bottles per spirit cover the 30-person scenario; three per spirit cover 45; four cover 60. If buying 1.75 L sizes, recompute total volume and price per ounce.

For 45 guests / 45 of each cocktail, multiply each 20-serving recipe by **2.25**. This gives approximately:
- Bourbon 2.0 L and gin 2.0 L.
- Cranberry cocktail 6.66 L total.
- Apple cider 2.0 L.
- Tart cherry juice 2.66 L.
- Lemon juice 1.33 L.
- Rosemary syrup 0.666 L.
- Ginger beer 2.0 L.
- Club soda 1.33 L.
- Chilled water 2.0 L in the recipes.

NA versions require additional ingredients. Start with 12–20 NA servings across both styles plus plenty of water/seltzer; adjust after RSVPs.

## Service plan
- Two labeled bowls/dispenser vessels; borrow before buying. A 20-serving batch is 4.44 L without ice: use at least roughly 6 L usable vessel capacity, or make 10-serving refills.
- Batch in small chilled reserves, not one giant room-temperature supply.
- Serve a measured 7.5 oz recipe portion into a larger cup with space for ice. Mark the ladle or use a measured pitcher.
- Add carbonation only to the amount being served.
- Keep NA drinks clearly distinct and use separate tools. Water gets the easiest access.
- Separate decorative moss/branches from food-contact surfaces and garnishes.
- Keep bagged ice and clean scoop separate from cooling ice.
- Suggested starting ice procurement: 1–1.5 lb per attendee across serving/chilling, with a nearby refill plan; revise for actual coolers, warm apartment and weather. At 45 guests that is about 45–68 lb, not a quantity to store in the freezer without checking space.
- Use smaller replenished batches and proper cold storage for juices/food; keep labels with ingredients.
