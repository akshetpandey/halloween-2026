# Bedroom • The Hollow
Status: Needs layout and bed/storage decision. Owner: host + two build helpers TBD.

## Guest experience
A root chamber under the same forest: mostly open dance space, an ominous woodland guardian at the DJ station, dark windows, restrained red/violet light. The “cave” can be suggested by shadow and hanging folds; a literal rock build is unnecessary.

## Bed decision
The original concept was to tie the bed vertically to the wall. Do not use an improvised rope/strap restraint to hold an ordinary bed over or beside guests. A standing bed is a major load and fall hazard; ordinary wall fixings and unknown furniture joints are not an engineered wall-bed system.

Preferred order:
1. Dismantle the frame following its manufacturer instructions, label/bag hardware, and store parts and mattress flat in a genuinely separate secured space.
2. If that space does not exist, keep the bed flat against a wall with a tight dark cover, shrink the dance area, and put the guardian on its own low stable support.
3. A proper wall-bed mechanism is only relevant if already installed and approved for that bed—not an event-night DIY project.

The host’s freedom to move furniture resolves permission, but does not establish structural suitability.

## Layout
- DJ table on a wall that is not the main through-route. Keep gear accessible from one side.
- Guardian behind or beside the table, never attached to the laptop/controller or unsupported mattress.
- Aim speakers inward toward listeners and away from windows/shared boundaries where possible.
- Keep speaker stands stable and outside the dance footprint; protect cables along the perimeter.
- Leave a lit exit route and sufficient access to windows/ventilation. Final dimensions depend on the room.
- No drink storage on the equipment table; make a separate drop surface outside the dance area.

## Draping with large windows
Measure each window and actual usable mounting span before buying fabric. Start with **one dark DJ backdrop plus the bedroom windows**. Host confirms no curtain rods. Current proposal is clipped fabric panels on suitable removable wall hooks; exact substrate, weight and mounting still need checking. Black velvet throughout is expensive, heavy and warm; matte black opaque fabric or borrowed curtains can achieve much of the same effect.

For decorative pleats allow roughly 1.5 times the covered width; choose panel count from actual product width. Include mounting, hems and floor clearance. Keep fabric clear of heaters, vents, equipment cooling and exit hardware. Use removable hooks only after verifying surface compatibility and the actual lightweight panel load; heavy velvet needs stronger support. Do not permanently black out the living room if selective branch silhouettes provide a better result.

## Floor and sound
Interlocking soft foam is not the default dance surface: seams, heels, spills and sliding can create problems. A firm existing floor is often the better starting point. Test any rug/pad for traction and edge stability with the actual shoes guests will wear.

Floor padding is not a reliable bass-soundproofing solution. Prioritize less bass, speakers off shared structure where feasible, inward aim, volume checks and a responsive host. See [apartment operations](../../operations/apartment.md).
