## What changes in this batch

Five lanes of ten are interleaved: **club hooks/vocal house, angular electro/dance-punk, melodic/sensual synth hooks, percussive/global bounce, and dark/playful club vocals**. The broader rhythmic and timbral palette tests the request for more variation. Vocal hooks remain central; atmospheric music must still move. This living-room experiment does not adopt the bedroom techno brief.

There are **32 new lead artists and 18 returning leads**, with one selected recording per lead and one per album ID. “New lead” does not mean no earlier collaboration. New leads include Dom Dolla, Honey Dijon, Soulwax, Róisín Murphy, Yaeji, Buraka Som Sistema, Noga Erez and Sudan Archives. Different songs by previously rejected artists are a small number of deliberate boundary tests, never alternate edits of a rejected composition. Classic by The Knocks and a few warmer house selections deliberately test whether a stronger hook can overcome the prior smoother-disco pattern.

All **291 prior library records**, including pending tracks, were excluded by URI and ISRC; normalized same-artist composition matching excludes alternate edits/remasters. The initial candidate screen caught and removed three already reviewed songs before this file was created. No title identifies an intro, outro, interlude or prelude. One selected song, House Of Jealous Lovers, exceeds five minutes at 5:05; its development has to justify the length. There are six explicit records. Full listening still needs to catch dead space, repetitive hooks, lyric fit and unsuitable edits.

The review order alternates styles for comparison; it is not an Automix sequence. The raw duration is **3:06:27**, or **2:56:39** after hypothetical 12-second overlaps. Those overlaps are planning arithmetic, not tested mix points. This batch alone does not solve the living-room nine-hour shortfall.
