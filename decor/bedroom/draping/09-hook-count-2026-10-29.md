**Current recommendation, October 29:** host confirms **window glass immediately below the ~1.5-in top band**, direct sun and only a few days’ use, then suggests suction hangers. ## Fitting and trial

1. Place the whole suction cup on clean, smooth **bare glass**, clear of frame joints, seals, texture and film. Keep the hook upright; never bridge two panes or mount on the ceiling.
2. For each 90-in blanket, start with **three points** across the top (left, middle, right); add a fourth for sag/edges if needed. Adjust to actual panes rather than a rigid 45-in grid. Six blankets need **18–24 hooks**, leaving **0–6 spare** from two packs. These are spacing proposals, not a load certification.
3. Grip the folded top with binder clips and place their wire handles on the suction hooks. Lower the top as needed to clear the frame, then fold excess from the 108-in drop so the hem stays off the floor. Keep removal tabs accessible and do not pull fabric taut.
4. **Test one intact blanket overnight, including a period with daylight on the glass, before hanging the rest.** Check for suction release, slipping clips and sag; reseat/check before guests arrive. Actual pane fit, temperature/condensation behavior and grip remain untested.

Suction works only where glass is available. This is not proof that every one of the six panels can reach suitable panes around all ~43 ft of wall. Tape-map the actual panel tops first; ordinary hooks may still serve a suitable solid vertical wall elsewhere, but **no extra wall-hook pack is currently ordered or counted**. No loading of blind rails or narrow trim assumed.
