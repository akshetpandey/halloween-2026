This batch tests ten tracks per lane. The order alternates lanes for taste calibration; large tempo/style changes make it unsuitable as an unattended DJ sequence.

The older V1 was rejected as a collection. That is not an individual downvote on every V1 recording. Four compositions are deliberate anchors from the unreviewed local V2: Josh Wink — Higher State of Consciousness; Amelie Lens — Hypnotized; Charlotte de Witte — Sgadi Li Mi; Space 92 — The Door. Genre lanes and fit/risk notes are editorial hypotheses; no full audio audition or numeric listening scores are claimed.

Keep/cut freely. No track is an intro/interlude/outro title, but full club versions can still have long DJ intros, outros and breakdowns.
