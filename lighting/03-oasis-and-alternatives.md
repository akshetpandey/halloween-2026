## Fixture strategy and one-corner test
Reuse suitable E26 lamps if their shades and shape work even when their current bulbs do not. Otherwise source two small stable wall-facing uplights plus a shaded lamp; example fixture category: [Hampton Bay 8-inch E26 uplight](https://www.homedepot.com/p/324482194), price not verified. Confirm bulb clearance, ventilation, fixture rating and simple on/off operation; do not assume smart bulbs work behind an existing mains dimmer.

Before committing the room:
1. Place one candidate behind furniture facing a pale wall, then try it inside the intended shade.
2. Compare low warm settings at seated eye level: glare, visible flicker, buzzing, sudden jumps and whether the lowest setting is still too bright.
3. Check faces, brown branches and green fabric/turf. A comfortable warm white should not make everyone look orange; use richer colors on background surfaces.
4. Try the daily controls, reset to useful white and power-loss behavior. Verify local/physical fallback rather than relying on cloud marketing language.
5. For scheduling, check slow scene changes and whether adaptive routines override the party preset. No automation code until the exact product/interface is selected.

Phone slow-motion video may reveal some flicker but is not a calibrated flicker test or a way to prove a lamp flicker-free. Published specs and a home trial are separate kinds of evidence. We have not established long-term comparative reliability or verified Oasis marketing/product expenditure.
