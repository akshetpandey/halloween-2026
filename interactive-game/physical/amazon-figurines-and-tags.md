## Base size and assembly

Use a **3-in / 76 mm base as the starting point**, with an unobstructed 40–45 mm circular target near the front edge, away from the feet. This accommodates roughly 25–40 mm tags without trimming. A 2-in / 51 mm base leaves little decorative margin with a 40 mm tag; it is not the preferred universal size. For a stag or moose, increase to roughly a 4–5-in oval if its measured stance demands it. Figure length, foot span and guest phone clearance are separate checks.

Place the complete tag flat on top of a base or in a shallow pocket, then conceal it under a thin removable nonmetal face. Start near 1–2 mm of cover and test; thickness is a prototype target, not a guaranteed reading distance. A layered cardboard/wood plinth can make the pocket without drilling the figure. Keep the tag near the guest-facing surface, rather than under a thick wood disk that guests must lift. Do not cut, drill, bake or hot-cast the tag. Put only one tag at each target.

Use **premade moon stickers**, as already requested; no Cricut or hand-cut moon workaround. A crescent alone does not cover a circular tag: use an opaque premade round backing large enough for the selected tag. Existing [moon sourcing leads](moon-touchpoints.md) still need a suitable delivered product and visibility/scan test. No new verified Amazon moon pack or base SKU is claimed here. Bases can use owned cardboard/wood; bought blanks remain within the materials allowance and must be measured.
