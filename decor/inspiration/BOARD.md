## Saved web references

### 01 • Pumpkin, moss and fallen leaves

![Pumpkin, moss and fallen leaves](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/pumpkin-forest.jpg)

**Take from this:** Use a pumpkin nestled into one contained moss tray. The layered texture is the useful detail; indoor implementation uses LED light.

**Source:** [Jiri Ikonomidis / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/spooky-jack-o-lantern-in-autumn-forest-setting-29359530/). **Use:** Pexels license. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 02 • A quieter harvest focal point

![A quieter harvest focal point](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/pumpkin-moss.jpg)

**Take from this:** Simple pumpkin shape, muted woodland background and negative space. A strong reference for the living-room palette.

**Source:** [Declan Conor / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/rustic-pumpkin-on-mossy-log-in-autumn-28787219/). **Use:** Pexels license. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 03 • Forest-floor scale and texture

![Forest-floor scale and texture](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/mushroom-moss.jpg)

**Take from this:** Small mushrooms and fine moss feel more woodland than oversized bright plastic props. Recreate in a tabletop fairy ring.

**Source:** [Jürgen / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/autumn-forest-scene-with-mushroom-and-moss-36424435/). **Use:** Pexels license. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 04 • Dark folds under red light

![Dark folds under red light](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/red-light-drapes.jpg)

**Take from this:** Borrow the principle of red light skimming dark fabric. This is a large professional event; our version is one bedroom backdrop and two lights.

**Source:** [VAN WYCK publisher; photographer not confirmed](https://www.vanwyck.net/). **Use:** Reference only; reuse rights not established. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 05 • Bark face and branch crown

![Bark face and branch crown](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/forest-mask.jpg)

**Take from this:** Useful for the Rootkeeper’s face and vertical silhouette. Keep the party prop lighter and guest costume antlers much smaller.

**Source:** [Pinterest pin linking to an Etsy mask; original maker not confirmed](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/cernunnos-green-man-mask-etsy--843228730224275440/). **Use:** Reference only; reuse rights not established. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 06 • Leaf layering and earthy clothing

![Leaf layering and earthy clothing](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/dryad.jpg)

**Take from this:** Leaf trims and earthy fabric do most of the work. Guests can borrow one detail without making a complete festival costume.

**Source:** [Pinterest event photograph; photographer not confirmed](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/elf-fantasy-fare-arcen-2018--612348880574160945/). **Use:** Reference only; retain visible credit/watermark. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 07 • One memorable headpiece

![One memorable headpiece](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/leaf-crown.jpg)

**Take from this:** Foliage, berries and an asymmetrical crown read immediately as woodland. Simplify to a soft lightweight headband.

**Source:** [Pinterest/Etsy reference; maker not independently confirmed](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/druid-horns-headdress-druid-costume-acsessories-pagan-headpiece-fairy-forest-witch-woodland-crown-with-fantasy-horns-deer-antler-headband-etsy--132082201562288161/). **Use:** Reference only; reuse rights not established. Saved September 3, 2026.

### 08 • Forest deity in bronze and moss

![Forest deity in bronze and moss](/Users/akshet/workspace/halloween-2026/decor/inspiration/images/forest-king.jpg)

**Take from this:** Moss green with aged gold makes a richer palette than bright Halloween colors. Use the color/material idea, not a costly complete outfit.

**Source:** [SETAdesignStudio / Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1856331145/mardi-gras-costume-forest-king-costume). **Use:** Reference only; reuse rights not established. Saved September 3, 2026.
