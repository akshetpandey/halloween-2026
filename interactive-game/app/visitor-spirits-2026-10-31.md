Updated October 31, 2026. Original authored artwork, guided by the host's private live reference photographs. These are public-safe geometry and vertex colors, with no photograph pixels or external models. This brief owns the four purchased animal visitors; the existing fox and gold tree remain separate.

Approximate dimensions were not supplied. Front is the face; the stage uses +Y up and +Z front, with the neutral base/feet on y=0. Photographs remain under ignored `private/live-guardians-2026-10-31/` and were inspected individually; no room photos are part of the public artwork.

The five-clip contract is identical across the visitors: `Idle` loops 2.4 seconds, `Awaken` plays 0.9 seconds, `Play` plays 3.0 seconds, `Settle` plays 1.0 second, then `Greet` loops 2.4 seconds. Idle is the emergence pose from stage time 0–2.4; anticipation occupies 2.4–3.3; the species action occupies 3.3–6.3; recovery/bow occupies 6.3–7.3; greeting follows. Every once clip joins the previous pose, and loop endpoints close. The full performance is authored joint/root motion inside each GLB, with the app supplying only the common entrance/stage.

Use closed toy-like surfaces and overlapping shoulder/neck/leg parts. Wings, beaks, heads, toes, antlers, ears and feet have explicit pivots; there is no detached photo cutout or synchronized floating substitute. Avoid a generic brown deer, generic white bunny, white snowy owl, or copying the fox tail chase. Keep each file below 1 MB, 18,000 triangles and 45 primitives where practical.
