## Why fleece / expected sound effect

Use one visible layer on every wall, including removable sections over the windows and closets. Let it hang with shallow folds and a little separation from the wall where the existing geometry allows. This is a reasonable modest reflection/echo treatment compared with bare walls; it is a design inference, **not an NRC or dB result**. Neither candidate has a verified acoustic rating or bass-isolation claim. The 40-yd design has near-flat coverage, not deep acoustic pleating. Soft feel, opacity and sound effect need the actual full-height trial.

No separate pads, second full-room lining or second desk-cloth order. If the window trial shows too much light, select a single more opaque material before committing rather than silently rebuilding a two-layer system. Existing blinds may remain useful.
