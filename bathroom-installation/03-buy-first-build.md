## Assembly, not sculpture
1. Lay owned dark cloth in the clean, dry tub, with soft protection beneath hard bases.
2. Set the moon in its supplied stand, clearly visible through the partly open forest curtain. Keep it on a stable dry base, accessible to the host but away from taps/splashes.
3. Bend the vine around the base as roots. Tuck 1–2 sheer cloth panels around it for texture; do not cover the light or its controls.
4. Set the covered gift container on a stable reachable dry shelf/caddy separate from the scenic materials.
5. Print/cut the existing blessing slips and make up the little gifts. Use the card from the main plan.

Estimated hands-on scenic assembly: 30–45 minutes once dimensions and supplies are settled. Add 30–45 minutes for printing and assembling roughly 60–72 gifts. These are planning estimates, not timed tests.

## Lighting gate
Charge the moon outside the bathroom and run it cordless for the installation. Before selecting it as the all-night centerpiece, test at the chosen brightness for 9 hours and check any automatic shutoff. If it fails, use it as an unlit textured prop under the shared battery candles or return to the printed moon; do not solve it with a charging lead across the bathroom. Do not assume water resistance.

The [Uncle Milton Moon In My Room](https://www.unclemilton.com/ever-wonder/moon_in_my_room/) was considered but not shortlisted as the primary light because the manufacturer specifies automatic shutoff. The rechargeable Mydethun options still need their own runtime/shutoff check; they have not been tested.
