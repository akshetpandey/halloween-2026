# TV shorts · Batch 03: strange worlds and drawn dreams

Researched September 8, 2026. **Reviewed: four approved, six rejected.** [Current brief, approvals and exclusions](tv-visuals.md) remain authoritative. No films acquired, cut or added to Jellyfin; no complete muted screenings or TV tests performed.

The host added *Fantastic Planet / La Planète sauvage* (1973) as a visual reference. Interpret this as surreal illustrated worlds, unusual creatures and dream logic; the feature itself stays outside the shorts-only programme. The first three picks explore that direction. The rest extend the existing drawn, woodland and arthouse preferences. The notes below preserve the original source-based audition rationale. Host verdicts in the main plan supersede those recommendations; no reasons were supplied for the six cuts.

## Source and access notes

[Machine-readable runtime evidence](tv-shorts-batch-03-metadata.json) records individual duration bases and access limitations. Checked source pages and metadata, not successful end-to-end playback from Brooklyn. Throat Notes and DONKS use festival runtimes; The Monk and the Fish uses its publisher's catalogue runtime. Their exact YouTube upload lengths remain to check.

- **Les Escargots:** [Criterion](https://www.criterionchannel.com/les-escargots) establishes the Laloux/Topor connection and 11:25 runtime; [Cinémathèque synopsis](https://www.cinematheque.fr/film/69155.html) describes the gigantic crops/snails and destruction. Sources disagree on 1965/1966, so the audition table omits the year.
- **Colgrave:** [Throat Notes at AIAF](https://www.aiaf.info/film/throat-notes-australia-2020) lists 8:30; [Annecy](https://www.annecyfestival.com/about/archives/2021/2021-programme/2021-index%3Afilm_pps-20211983) describes the premise and 2D technique. [ASIFA's programme](https://asifa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2023-november-december-asifa-central-newsletter-22-page-assembly-with-appendices.pdf) lists DONKS at 6:36; [Colgrave's soundtrack notes](https://felixcolgrave.bandcamp.com/album/donks-ost) explain the plastic-body ecosystem. YouTube oEmbed confirmed both titles and the creator as uploader. The soundtrack's 24:41 is not the film runtime.
- **Hairy Hill:** creator Vimeo metadata gives 1,034 seconds; [Annecy](https://www.annecyfestival.com/en/return-hairy-hill) confirms 17:14 and mixed animation techniques; [Durban festival](https://ccadiff.ukzn.ac.za/diff45/return-to-hairy-hill/) identifies no dialogue and the animal transformations.
- **NFB:** exact page metadata and/or collection records support [Here and the Great Elsewhere](https://collection.nfb.ca/film/here_and_the_great_elsewhere), [Mr. Samsa](https://collection.nfb.ca/film/metamorphosis_of_mr_samsa), [Imprints](https://collection.nfb.ca/film/imprints) and [Rains](https://collection.nfb.ca/film/rains). Mindscape's film-page metadata gives 451 seconds. Rains uses NFB's 7:43 version rather than Folimage's 7:40 listing.
- **Monk:** [Folimage](https://www.folimage.fr/en/films/the-monk-and-the-fish-49.htm) confirms 6:30, paper drawings/painting, no dialogue and a VOD route.

These links are audition routes, not acquired local media. Acquisition/storage/editing status remains as documented in the [main plan](tv-visuals.md). No subscription or film purchase was made.

## Research held back

Rhizome has a promising ink/watercolour aesthetic, but the verified creator/studio Vimeo links were trailers; it is not counted. Madame Tutli-Putli, Scavengers and The Old Lady and the Pigeons were not advanced because their darker violence/body-horror material risks repeating the Skin for Skin mismatch. These are sourcing choices, not additional host rejections.
