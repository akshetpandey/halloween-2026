# Optional story and interaction • The Last Leaf
Status: Proposal ready. Priority P2. Owner: host + 2 volunteers if available. Target budget: $20 in working plan.
Complexity ceiling: one sentence of lore, three short beats, no puzzles required to enjoy the party.

## Premise
The Rootkeeper has kept one leaf alive after the harvest. The court must decide what to release and what to carry into winter.

Guests can participate quietly or ignore it. Nobody is singled out, touched, isolated, frightened on purpose or asked to reveal personal information.

## Always-on interaction: the offering bowl
Put paper leaf shapes, pencils and a lidded dark box/bowl beside a soft amber light. Prompt:
**“Write something you’re ready to leave in the old season. Fold it. The forest keeps its own counsel.”**

Guests place folded leaves inside. Nobody reads them. After the party, the host disposes of them unread. A second small card invites a hope for winter, which can remain private too.

This replaces the initial flash-paper idea. With hanging cloth, paper, branches, alcohol and costumes, use the symbolic act and lighting change without combustion. NFPA recommends battery lights for Halloween decorations. [NFPA](https://content.nfpa.org/es/education-and-research/home-fire-safety/halloween)

## Three story beats
**Arrival, ongoing — the Keeper of Leaves.** A volunteer offers a paper leaf token:
“Welcome to the Hollow Court. Keep a leaf if you like. Before winter, the forest asks what you’re ready to leave behind.”

**Around 11:45 p.m. EDT — a quiet invitation.** Host and volunteer invite small groups to the offering station while normal music continues:
“The last leaf is ready to fall. If there’s something you’d like to leave with the forest, the bowl is here.”

**Around 12:15 a.m. EDT — the turning, maximum 60–90 seconds.** The host says:
“What we leave stays with the roots. What we carry belongs to the living. The court is open.”

A helper dims the amber altar light and turns on the guardian eyes / changes the bedroom scene. Music transitions into the next prepared block. No blackout and no pressure for everyone to assemble.

Optional 3 a.m. EST closing card: “Take your name, your coat, and whatever the forest gave you.”

## Minimal staffing
Host: introduces the moment.  
Volunteer 1: offers tokens and keeps passage clear.  
Volunteer 2 or audio helper: makes one lighting/music change.  
If only the host is available, keep the bowl and drop the performance entirely.

## Props
Paper leaf shapes, two pencils, lidded opaque box, two small printed cards, one battery light. Reuse scenery.
