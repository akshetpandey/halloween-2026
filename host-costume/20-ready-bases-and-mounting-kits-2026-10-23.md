## Why the Shed Spreader is only a partial shortcut

[Manufacturer product page](https://illusionsystems.com/collections/deer-antler-mounts/products/shed-spreader): molded polymer, two sizes and included screws. Page lists **0.5 lb kit weight**, not the worn mass of one selected plate. Amazon lists **9.6 × 5.6 × 2.5 inches**; do **not** treat that as verified individual mounting footprint or antler spacing. Exact seat/base dimensions and selected assembled weight are not established. The vendor's own page mixes “Sold Out” template text with a stock figure; Amazon's live purchasable selected offer is the stock/delivery evidence used here, not that contradictory page.

In principle it removes making two separate antler seats and supplies a common bridge; the cap removes making/fitting a headband from raw leather/metal. However:

1. **Antler-to-plate:** pilot drilling and orientation remain. Fixed pedestal geometry may flare the natural set outward or require base preparation, affecting the requested narrow upright silhouette. Do not assume “universal” means an exact cosmetic fit.
2. **Plate-to-cap:** no compatible bolt-on adaptor/ready holes were found. The plate must seat without rocking and connect at separated mechanical points with appropriate load spreading. Exact positions/fasteners require physical dimensions; this is not a drilling recipe. The proposed generic screw/washer scheme is not manufacturer-tested headwear.
3. **Cap-to-head:** the suspension fits a head; it is not evidence it retains tall antlers while bending/turning. Retention, pressure and total mass require an actual test. No safe universal antler-mass threshold invented.
4. **Veil:** neither product includes the elevated lightweight canopy support, fabric or independent release. That remains part of the two-hour allowance, not a hidden separate project. Veil lifted/off for walking, serving or dancing.

The Shed Spreader's published mechanical assembly does not specify adhesive; that **may avoid an adhesive-curing step for the shed-to-plate connection**. It does not establish a glue-free, ready-compatible plate-to-cap connection or full headpiece. Do not shorten the previous estimated build time simply because these components are in stock. No new validated assembly duration.

**Decision:** shortlist the Shed Spreader and adjustable bump cap as low-cost prototype candidates, **not a final shopping instruction**. Do not buy the cap solely to drill it without a resolved mounting plan. Wait for antler mass/base dimensions and reliable shipping; then choose whether the specific plate geometry reduces work or creates an unnecessary extra joint. Direct antler-to-base attachment may be simpler in some cases; no blanket claim this display kit saves time for every set.
