## Layering with the two looks

- **Eclipse:** black jeans, mesh tank and gold chain remain the core. Short black fur frames the chest and gives the halo a darker silhouette. A thin black long-sleeve base is the more covered/cold-friendly alternative; keep chain over the base so the outer jacket removes independently. Do not attach chain to fur or put gold mesh over shaggy pile: snag risk.
- **King:** green trousers, beige lace-up shirt, compact bone-look crown and gold netting remain. H&M's brown collar can bridge the woodland palette. Fold the pale shoulder cloth short, secure without restricting the zipper, and keep the gold face panel off while moving. Faux fur could also work with King, but leave the netting clear of its pile.
- Outerwear may be closed for cold transit; headpiece still signals the look. Gloves and warm socks matter for the return trip. No decorative jacket, PUFFTECH or thermal has been physically tested. Follow the [overnight plan](07-cold-weather-layers-2026-10-21.md), especially for possible 4–9 a.m. travel.
