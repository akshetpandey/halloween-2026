# Come as something the forest remembers
Pagan gods, woodland creatures, harvest spirits, witches and fae all belong here. You can invent your own.

[Explore the sourced art inspiration boards](COSTUME-ART-BOARD.md) and [the new Yellowjackets / ritual costume references](COSTUME-RITUAL-BOARD.md). Borrow a silhouette, texture or palette; you do not need to recreate an entire painting or buy a complete costume.

| Look | Clothes you may already own | One defining detail |
|---|---|---|
| Antler oracle / woodland celebrant | Ivory knit, robe or layered outfit | Compact antler crown; optional short lace veil |
| Dusk-crowned monarch | Dark shawl or cloak over ordinary clothes | Small aged-gold halo headband |
| Forest god / Green Man | Brown or green shirt, trousers, boots | Leaf collar or small soft antlers |
| Harvest goddess / deity | Burgundy, gold or cream dress, robe or layers | Apple/leaf crown or fruit-shaped jewelry |
| Moon spirit | Grey, black or silver layers | Crescent pendant and silver makeup |
| Mushroom sprite | Cream shirt and earthy trousers/skirt | Soft mushroom beret or spotted cap |
| Woodland witch | Black clothes and green shawl | Twig/leaf brooch or a small charm pouch |
| Faun | Brown trousers, boots, open shirt or vest | Small curved horns |
| Dryad | Earthy clothes with leaf accents | Bark-like makeup on one cheek |
| Moth / night spirit | Dark layers or a shawl | Moth-eye pattern or antenna headband |
| River / well spirit | Blue-grey or silver fabric | Pearl-like accents or a rippling scarf |
| The last autumn leaf | Rust, copper and brown layers | One oversized paper leaf |

**Low effort:** wear the palette and add one symbol.  
**A little crafting:** leaf crown, mushroom hat, bark mask or embroidered-looking paper collar.  
**Go elaborate:** create your own forest deity and give them a name.

No need to buy a complete costume. Keep antlers/wings compact, hems clear of the floor, masks easy to see through, and shoes comfortable enough to dance in.

Any look can be worn by anyone.
