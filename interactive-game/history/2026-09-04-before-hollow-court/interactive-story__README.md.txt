# The succession of the Hollow Court
Status: New host-proposed direction; feasibility researched, no website built or deployed. Lower priority than room setup and music. September 3, 2026.

## Premise
The Antler King’s season is ending. The forest has hidden witnesses to his reign. Those who find their marks may lay a claim to the next crown.

The host begins as the reigning **Antler King**. Two or three volunteers spread fragments of the story through brief conversations and actions. Guests discover the hunt inside the party; **no succession plot, scavenger hunt or scoring language goes on the invite**.

## Recommended version
Latest proposal: 10–15 fixed bark spirits across public party areas, each with a separate moon-covered NFC URL tag immediately below/beside it; twelve is a planning midpoint. No QR codes. Helper-assisted claims are the proposed exception for unsupported phones. A guest chooses a nickname once, then receives one point per distinct totem claimed. The totems stay in place. At a stated cutoff, the highest score wins; a ceremonial draw settles ties among willing, present leaders. The host offers a separate simple crown or medallion.

This keeps the user’s “most finds wins” idea. Final scoring, number of totems and timing remain proposals. Participation is optional, and guests can enjoy the stories without competing.
