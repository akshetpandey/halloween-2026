## Look to pursue, not a fully sourced basket

- Two relatively narrow, upright **natural-brown forked antler clusters**, with the fork tips visible above the veil. Not a broad white stag fan, floral headband, skull tiara or flat low circlet.
- **Textured old-gold crochet/open knit** forming a high canopy and covering the whole face to the upper chest. It should not merely fall behind the head or start at the forehead like a bridal veil.
- **Matte warm-ivory layered robe/hood/shoulder cloth** for the reference's ceremonial silhouette. This is a styling recommendation, not a newly selected or deadline-verified garment. Green trousers and shoes can remain underneath; the full pale drape should carry the look. The weekend's black fur/mesh/torc is not automatically the screen-close body costume.
- No self-applied face makeup. Keep the face veil independently liftable/removable. Wear it lowered for photos/a brief ceremony, not while serving drinks, walking through a crowd or dancing.

The saved [real reference](references/antler-queen.jpg) was inspected again. Its geometry is a visual reference, **not an authenticated species identification or measured pattern**. [Costume designer Marie Schley](https://theartofcostume.com/2025/06/11/rewriting-the-rules-of-survival-fashion-marie-schley-on-costumes-in-yellowjackets-season-3/) describes a crocheted 1990s dress as the original veil source.
