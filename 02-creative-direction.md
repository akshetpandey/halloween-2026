# Creative direction • The Hollow Court
Status: Ready to review. Owner: host. Decision target: September 5.

## Premise
Once a year, the forest opens a court for its gods, ghosts and creatures. Above ground, they feast in the last warmth of autumn. Beneath the roots, they dance until the season turns.

**The Hollow Court** is the recommended title. Alternatives: **The Last Harvest** (more folk horror), **Beneath the October Moon** (more enchanted).

The invented story borrows seasonal harvest, liminal-night and woodland-folklore imagery. It is pop-culture fantasy, not a reconstruction of a particular religious practice. Fairy rings provide a natural story device; the Woodland Trust documents their associations with fairies and dancing. [Reference](https://www.woodlandtrust.org.uk/blog/2019/08/what-is-a-fairy-ring/)

## One world, three rooms
| Space | Emotional job | Dominant treatment | Repeated symbol |
|---|---|---|---|
| October Grove / living room | Welcome, talk, drink, discover | Moss green, pumpkin amber, pale moon | Leaf + moon |
| The Hollow / bedroom | Release, movement, darker mystery | Charcoal, oxblood, muted violet | Roots + antlers |
| Moon Well / bathroom | Pause and reset | Silver, warm white, small green accent | Moon reflected in water |

## Visual rules
- Five recurring materials: branch silhouettes, moss texture, fallen-leaf shapes, dark cloth, warm pinpoints of light.
- Palette: moss #3D4A32; oxblood #651F2B; charcoal #161916; amber #C88A3B; moon ivory #E6E0CC.
- Make the living room roughly two parts enchanting to one part unsettling. The bedroom gets its unease from shadow and rhythm.
- Repeat the same moon/leaf/root motifs on cocktail labels, room names and optional tokens.
- Cluster detail at eye level and at scenic edges. Clear floors should read as intentional negative space.
- Use one strong moon and one guardian face as memorable anchors.
- Avoid filling every surface. A few deep shadows make modest scenery feel more substantial.

## Pop-culture reference vocabulary
**The Green Knight:** ancient timber, medieval mystery, rich green/gold shadow. [A24](https://a24films.com/films/the-green-knight)  
**The Secret of Kells / Wolfwalkers / Song of the Sea:** graphic foliage, glowing spirits and approachable folklore. [Cartoon Saloon](https://www.cartoonsaloon.ie/irish-animation-studio/feature-films/)  
**Ents / dryads / fae courts:** recognizable costume shorthand supplied by the brief.  
**Underground club:** limited light palette, hidden light sources, dark backdrop, silhouette.

These are mood references, not claims about what is trending in autumn 2026. Refresh guest-facing references in October if desired.

## Spend for impact
First: controlled lighting, window treatment, forest focal point, guardian silhouette.  
Second: tactile tabletop detail, good signage and drink color.  
Last: ceiling coverage, full-wall velvet, complicated mechanisms and a long story.
