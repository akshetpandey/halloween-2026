Cloudflare is trusted for identity issuance and policy; a stolen valid JWT could be replayed until expiry. The HTTP hop between the two containers is on the local Docker network. Other local processes or containers joined to that network can reach the port, but still need a valid JWT. A denied external identity and a second authorized reviewer have not yet been tested end to end.
