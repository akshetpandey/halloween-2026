# October Grove · Batch 05: sharp hooks

Prepared September 22, 2026. **50 recordings, 50 distinct lead artists, 2:57:40 raw.** [Spotify audition](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/3XSBmeYNcXAfsqeqj7qMXL) is populated and read-back verified in the order below. [Portable batch data](october-grove-batch-05.json) records exact versions and sourced metadata without votes. The protected [Playlist Lab](https://music.hollow-court.com) now opens Batch 05 for host review.

## What the completed votes suggest

Batch 04 ended **12 keeps / 38 cuts (24% kept)**, after Batch 03’s 17/25 and Batch 02’s 12/25. The hosted library had **45 keeps / 196 cuts** before this import; all 50 new tracks start unrated. The host left no written reasons, so the pattern below is an editorial hypothesis rather than a reason attributed to them.

Batch 04 keeps cluster around assertive vocals, strong hooks, angular electro/dance-punk, theatrical synth-pop and tracks with a bolder rhythmic identity: Von dutch, 212, Acapella, Tribulations, Bizarre Love Triangle, Je veux te voir, Audacity of Huge and disco tits are useful examples. Smooth French/disco-house and several long, even-groove cuts mostly lost: Lady, Lola’s Theme, Music Sounds Better With You, Stupidisco, Groovejet and Digital Love were all cut. This does **not** imply that every disco or house record fails. Catalog BPM and energy overlap substantially between keeps and cuts (Batch 04 median BPM about 127 versus 124; median catalog energy 0.81 versus 0.80), so neither makes a reliable threshold.

## Selection experiment

Five lanes are interleaved every five tracks: **angular dance/punk, club hooks/electro, maximalist electropop, sexy rhythmic/rap-pop, and expressive wildcards**. The intent is to make the next decisions informative without forcing 50 near-identical records. There are **38 new lead artists and 12 returning leads**; eleven returning leads have at least one earlier keep, while Basement Jaxx is a deliberate different-song boundary test after Red Alert was cut. The mix is more vocal and punchy than Batch 04. Some famous pop tracks are here to test immediate party recognition; they are not presumed approved.

No previously reviewed Spotify URI, ISRC or same-lead song title/alternate edit recurs. The initial draft accidentally included full versions of two previously cut songs; those were removed before import. Each lead and album ID appears once in this batch. Collaborations still overlap: Charli xcx appears with Icona Pop and solo; Ciara and Missy Elliott share Lose Control / 1, 2 Step. Keep those appearances separated when programming a final sequence. Every selected version is under five minutes, and obvious intro/outro/interlude titles are excluded; full listening must still catch dead space, repetition or unsuitable lyrics.

## Metadata, playback and next step

Spotify identity, duration, and US playability were checked for **50/50**. ReccoBeats provides exact-ID or same-ISRC/title/artists/duration catalog descriptors for **47/50**; YouTube metadata candidates cover **43/50**. Artist-level Spotify genre records were fetched for **58 credited artists**, with nonempty genres on 34 tracks. Editorial lanes are track-level listening hypotheses, not Spotify genres. ReccoBeats descriptors are sourced catalog values, not independent audio measurements or predictions of the host’s votes. No credentials, private feedback or audio went to either metadata service.

Catalog descriptors remain unknown for We Are Rockstars, Banquet and Hustler. Spotify playback is the fallback for Deceptacon, Sweet Dreams, I Love It, How to Be a Heartbreaker, The Way I Are, Nasty and Vroom Vroom. The proposed YouTube result for I Love It explicitly identified a 2022 re-edit and was excluded. Remaining YouTube links are metadata candidates within five seconds of the Spotify duration; alternate versions or embedding failures still require review by ear.

Spotify’s API reports this audition playlist as `public: true` despite creation and a later update requesting `public: false`. Treat the Spotify link as shareable or discoverable until its visibility is confirmed in the Spotify client. This is separate from Playlist Lab: Cloudflare Access protects the review page and votes.
