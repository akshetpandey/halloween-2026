- Dance Dance's tiring-week setup and Do What You Do's early money worry resolve into enjoyment/confidence. Bad Boy Good Man has sexual/drug-slang swagger; Geraldines Routine is a flirtatious dancer/stripper story. No sad or revenge narrative was identified in those reviewed texts.
- **Love Is in Town (5:21)** is the longest candidate; its soul voice and sung/scat sequence must justify the length. April Showers, Suzy and Jus 1 Kiss also have exposed repetition risks. A happy lyric cannot redeem a bland or static recording.
