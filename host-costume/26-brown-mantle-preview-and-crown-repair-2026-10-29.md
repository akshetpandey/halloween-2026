## Crown material and glue

The exact [Sosation crown B0H69GX4HF listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H69GX4HF), checked October 29, explicitly says **PLA** and **3D printed**. It is a plastic bone-look crown. The host's actual item's polymer has not been independently tested, but the selected listing supports PLA as the working material.

**Recommendation for two small, clean, close-fitting decorative breaks: gel cyanoacrylate (CA) superglue, such as [Loctite Super Glue Ultra Gel Control](https://www.loctiteproducts.com/products/central-pdp.html/loctite-super-glue-ultra-gel-control/SAP_0201DGL029X9/variation/1801751.html).** [Prusa's firsthand print-assembly guide](https://blog.prusa3d.com/the-great-guide-to-gluing-and-assembling-3d-prints_44908/) supports CA for PLA and other usual print polymers; gel offers controlled placement. This is a repair recommendation, not a tested bond on this crown. No need for the retired headgear fabrication or large-antler mount.

1. Work with the crown **off the head**, on a protected surface with ventilation. Keep glue away from hair, skin and textiles; nitrile gloves are preferable to cotton. Dry-fit each piece first to establish its exact orientation and that edges meet without a gap.
2. Remove dust/grease gently and let surfaces dry completely. Preserve the matching fracture geometry; do not sand away the small locating edges or force the crown into a new shape.
3. Put a **tiny thin film on one mating face**, using a toothpick for control if needed. Fit once, hold still **15–30 seconds** according to the Loctite directions, and leave undisturbed at least **five minutes**. Prop the part so gravity does not bend the joint; repair one piece at a time. More glue is not a stronger repair.
4. Allow **24 hours for full cure before wearing or flexing**; plan to repair by Friday with adequate time before Saturday's party. About **15–20 minutes active work** for two simple pieces is a planning estimate, not a measured build; full curing is additional elapsed time. Then gently inspect the joints and do a movement/comfort trial without loading the tips.

[Henkel's Ultra Gel Control technical data sheet](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-Super-Glue-Ultra-Gel-Control-en_US.pdf) supplies the close-fitting/clean/dry preparation, 15–30-second fixture, at least five minutes undisturbed and full 24-hour cure. Avoid adding water, heat, acetone solvent-welding, cotton wrapping or accelerator for this simple repair. CA can react strongly with cotton/wool; the printed shape and adhesive joint should not be heat-reshaped. These precautions follow the material/adhesive guidance and our conservative handling choice, not a claim that the crown has passed a test.

This method assumes **small decorative fragments with snug mating edges**. A split in the load-bearing circlet, missing material or a joint that must flex is a different repair: simple CA can be weak under twisting/peeling, so reinforcement/replacement would need assessment. Broken locations are unknown; no guarantee the crown is structurally sound for wearing. Do not treat the render's intact crown as a completed repair.
