## Build and fit gates

1. **Before ordering:** head circumference at crown position; height; usual shirt size; and a quick inventory/photo of owned cream/black clothes, boots and a possible cream shawl. Confirm DIY tolerance and the approved total including shipping. No fitted clothing recommendation is needed yet.
2. **Crown:** trial unaltered first. Keep branches within the shoulder envelope where possible, with tips directed upward; check head turns, ceiling fixtures, hugging and reaching. Remove it for dancing if it shifts or catches. Seller “lightweight” claims are not a comfort test.
3. **Veil:** start with a single approximately 24–30-inch-wide front panel, length fitted from brow to upper chest. Reinforce its top with ribbon and use two accessible snaps/clips on the brow wrap; **do not suspend it from antler tips**. Keep it removable independently of the crown. If dim-light sightlines are poor, lift it immediately; no veiled walking, serving or dancing. No dyes, glue or paint on mesh near the face.
4. **Mantle:** shoulder drape, open arms, ends above hips, easy-release front fastening rather than a tight throat tie. Use existing oxblood ribbon/scrap if available. Keep cloth away from flames; no loose shedding moss or brittle twig decorations. Weathered texture can come from natural cream and folds without distressing an otherwise reusable piece.
5. **Rehearse:** 30–45 minutes talking, sitting, bending and dancing in actual room lighting. Pass only if hands, pockets, bathroom access, vision and removal are straightforward. Keep veil off except for an optional brief 60–90-second crowning moment.

If succession runs, volunteers privately convey “choosing the next king”; nothing goes on the invite. Winner receives a **separate simple soft leaf/twig-look crown**, not the host's fitted antlers. The three decor-cart raven skulls remain allocated to scenery; **none is included in this costume**.
