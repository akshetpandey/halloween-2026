## Setup guide for the host
Partiful’s current official guide supports a custom uploaded poster, event fields, RSVP limit, questions and “Save Draft.” Saving is separate from inviting guests. [Official guide](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525299-creating-your-first-partiful-event)

1. Host signs in to their own Partiful account and creates the event.
2. Fill title, date, confirmed hours and neighborhood; upload cover.
3. Add description. Put exact apartment entry details only in the chosen guest-visible location field.
4. Inspect current privacy/approval options. Prefer controlled attendance and a non-public address; verify the actual guest view before adding a street/unit address.
5. Decide plus-one rule and whether to hide the guest list. Partiful documents guest-list privacy and password protection. [Privacy guide](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525648-how-does-partiful-protect-my-data)
6. Add at most three RSVP questions: likely arrival window, dietary/access needs (private response if available), one music suggestion.
7. Preview on a phone. Confirm dates, image crop, readable text and address visibility.
8. Host saves the event and can add the real URL here later; host distributes it to selected recipients.

Do not assume an RSVP cap measures who is inside at once. Use arrival windows and a door helper if staggered attendance is important.
