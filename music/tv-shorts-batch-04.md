# TV shorts · Batch 04: forest rituals and strange creatures

Researched September 8, 2026. **Reviewed: six approved, four rejected.** The [main TV plan](tv-visuals.md) owns current verdicts and totals; the audition rationale below is historical and superseded by those verdicts. No media acquired or edited; full muted screenings and TV tests remain pending.

This batch follows the approved woodland transformations and illustrated surrealism, particularly Return to Hairy Hill, Throat Notes, Les Escargots and Here and the Great Elsewhere. No new blanket preference is inferred from the Batch 03–04 cuts. Fit assessments are recommendations based on creator/publisher descriptions and source evidence, not full-film screening results.

## Original audition notes · historical

Titles link to full-film viewing pages, with creator/studio upload metadata verified for the nine Vimeo films. The tenth is an NFB film page. These are audition routes; browser/account/regional playback and local-file acquisition remain untested.

| Short / creator | Runtime | Why try it | Muted fit and relevant caveat |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **[Nocturne / Nachtstück](https://vimeo.com/316074337)** — Anne Breymann | **5:19** | Strange woodland creatures gather at night to gamble with their innermost selves. Closest direct match to the eerie forest-party setting. | Handcrafted puppets and ritual actions; uncanny imagery. Sound design contributes atmosphere, but the visual premise is the main reason to audition it. |
| **[Chado](https://vimeo.com/391834727)** — Dominica Harrison | **7:29** | Enchanted Russian wilderness, a child's unsettling visions and richly textured risograph animation. A promising companion to Return to Hairy Hill. | Family disruption, an ill dog and a dangerous decision by the child. Evaluate the complete muted story; no claim of a content-clearance screening. |
| **[The Astronomer’s Dream](https://vimeo.com/5452888)** — Malcolm Sutherland | **11:30** | A hungry astronomer's subconscious becomes a bizarre cosmic food chain. A substantial illustrated dream-world option after Fantastic Planet and Throat Notes. | Surreal creature/body transformations and consumption. The recommendation is for the visual dream logic; test the complete film against the host's violence boundary. This is Sutherland's 2009 animation, not the Méliès film of the same name. |
| **[The Elephant’s Garden](https://vimeo.com/121218625)** — Felix Colgrave | **4:25** | A colourful psychedelic ecosystem by the creator of Throat Notes. | **[A Mind Sang](https://vimeo.com/430690235)** — Vier Nev | **6:11** | Figures, landscapes and shadows become different images as perspective shifts. Optical illusions and rebirth imagery make it a strong arthouse candidate. | The creator builds meaning into shapes/negative space, making it promising muted. Includes symbolic birth/death and red imagery representing blood; don't describe it as entirely gentle. |
| **[ZOON](https://vimeo.com/953072985)** — Jonatan Schwenk | **4:25** | Glowing axolotls and strange forest dwellers in a nocturnal swamp. An odd miniature ecology alongside Throat Notes and Island. | Distributor confirms no dialogue. Creatures nuzzle, nibble limbs and are eaten; stylized predation and sexual undertones merit a taste check. |
| **[Kukuschka](https://vimeo.com/157167697)** — Dina Velikovskaya | **8:43** | An unusual puppet bird follows the sun through a strange landscape, then acquires a companion. | **[The Man Without a Shadow / L’homme sans ombre](https://www.nfb.ca/film/homme_sans_ombre_en/)** — Georges Schwizgebel | **9:35** | A man trades his shadow to a magician; the world keeps flowing into fresh painted compositions. Folklore, magic and shadow theatre. | NFB explicitly identifies it as without words. A different visual treatment from the rejected Mindscape and Imprints; the host still needs to judge it. |
| **[Au revoir Jérôme!](https://vimeo.com/767548129)** — Chloé Farr, Gabrielle Selnet & Adam Sillard / GOBELINS | **7:44** | A search through an exuberant, constantly changing illustrated afterlife. A colourful surreal counterpart to O Black Hole! | French dialogue makes the plot a less certain muted fit; audition for the changing world and visual transformations. Theme includes death and separation. |
| **[Muedra](https://vimeo.com/297904352)** — César Díaz Meléndez | **8:55** | Clay, stones, plants and pinecones come alive in an unfamiliar natural world. A stop-motion nature fable. | The style wildcard: actual outdoor landscapes rather than drawn backgrounds. Deliberate object animation makes it worth comparing with the approved handmade films, but it may still feel too naturalistic. |

## Source evidence and access

[Structured metadata](tv-shorts-batch-04-metadata.json) records exact upload runtimes, uploader identity, viewing pages and supporting sources. Source pages and metadata were checked September 8; films were not played end to end.

- [Anne Breymann](https://www.annebreymann.de/work/nocturne) confirms Nocturne's forest-gambling premise and handmade production credits.
- [Chado's co-producer](https://www.blindpig.tv/portfolio/chado) describes the wilderness setting, visions, risograph technique and full online premiere. The linked Vimeo upload is the filmmaker's version.
- [Sutherland's upload](https://vimeo.com/5452888) identifies The Astronomer's Dream; [festival retrospective](https://bsf.si/media/publicity/text/68/68f68fc1c69cd14d3c7a1cab175dec1ad18baaad.pdf) describes the hungry astronomer and subconscious food chain.
- [QAGOMA's programme](https://www.qagoma.qld.gov.au/cinema/screening/animation-shorts-rich-and-infamous-2022-03-09) supports The Elephant's Garden's surreal ecology and 4:25 runtime; the viewing link is Colgrave's upload.
- [Vier Nev's interview](https://vimeo.com/blog/post/staff-pick-award-a-mind-sang-vier-nev) explains A Mind Sang's optical illusions, 2D animation and symbolic red/blood imagery.
- [ZOON's distributor](https://verleih.shortfilm.com/en/films/zoon) confirms no dialogue and describes the predation. Its 4:37 catalogue length differs from the creator's **4:25 upload**, which is used here.
- [Velikovskaya's upload](https://vimeo.com/157167697) identifies Kukuschka as puppet stop motion and describes the journey. Its description says 8:45; the actual oEmbed upload is **8:43**, used here.
- [NFB collection](https://collection.nfb.ca/film/homme_sans_ombre_en) and film-page runtime metadata agree on **9:35** for The Man Without a Shadow. The film page describes the freshly repainted cels and absence of dialogue.
- [Miyu](https://www.miyu.fr/distribution/au-revoir-jerome/) confirms the drawn/2D technique and afterlife premise for Au revoir Jérôme!; GOBELINS's full upload supplies the exact duration.
- [Muedra's making-of description](https://vimeo.com/369420941) explains the outdoor stop-motion process and animated natural objects. The recommendation links the separate full film, not this making-of or either trailer.

No purchase, download, storage/edit permission or Jellyfin playback readiness is implied by these viewing links. Existing acquisition constraints remain in the [main plan](tv-visuals.md).
