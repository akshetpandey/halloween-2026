# Woodland DJ guardian • The Rootkeeper
Status: Build proposal ready. Priority P1. Owner: build helper TBD. Materials allowance: $60 in the working budget. Build time estimate: 3–5 person-hours for a bust, plus drying time.

## Concept
An ancient branch spirit appears to operate the booth. A strong face silhouette and branch crown carry the effect; a complete life-size articulated creature adds work without much extra impact.

Recommended version: a lightweight bust behind the controller, with a faceted bark-like face, asymmetrical branch crown, dark draped torso and two dull amber points for eyes. Make it strange rather than comic. All size choices wait for DJ-table and ceiling measurements.

## Build plan
1. Mock the silhouette in cardboard at the proposed position.
2. Form a lightweight face from folded/corrugated cardboard and crumpled kraft paper. Paint charcoal first, then dry-brush muted brown and moss green.
3. Use paper tubes, lightweight faux twigs or a small number of clean, dry branches for the crown. Keep tips away from faces and walking routes.
4. Attach the bust to a stable low stand with a broad base and restrained weight low down. Have a competent helper choose connections appropriate to the actual stand/materials.
5. Add fabric shoulders and sparse attached moss. Keep material clear of laptop vents and all electrical equipment.
6. Use enclosed battery LEDs for eyes or a hidden external amber uplight. Make switches reachable.
7. Photograph in the actual light; add detail only where it can be seen.

## Suggested materials
Reused cardboard, kraft paper, matte paint, cloth offcuts, small faux moss pieces, cable ties/appropriate fasteners, battery LEDs and a borrowed stable support. Do not use the bed as the creature’s load-bearing mount.

## Optional upgrade
Thin paper-root silhouettes spread across the black backdrop behind the bust. This increases apparent scale without creating a large object. Budget $15–30 only if the main silhouette already works.
