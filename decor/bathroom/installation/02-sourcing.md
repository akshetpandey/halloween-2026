## Selection rule
The $16.59 curtain gives the strongest value if a new curtain is needed. Use the darker $32.99 version only if its product images and actual dimensions suit the bathroom. An existing dark curtain with the moon scene may be better than either purchase. Keep the existing shower liner for later ordinary use if appropriate.

The chocolate links establish realistic small-order quantities; they are not a reason to pay disproportionate shipping. Compare a local bag of wrapped chocolates during the main grocery shop. Amazon remains preferred when a matching product has a credible seller and better delivered cost, but no unverified Amazon ASIN is substituted here.
