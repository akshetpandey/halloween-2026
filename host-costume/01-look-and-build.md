# The Antler King • build direction v1
Proposal, September 3. Pop-culture woodland ceremony; no claim of historical accuracy.

## Recommended silhouette
**Weathered ivory king:** cream or oatmeal trousers with an oversized tunic/shirt; a short cape or asymmetrical shoulder mantle; soft branching antlers; a removable open-knit veil; a dark cord belt and one oxblood sash or ribbon. Use dark comfortable boots already owned. A broad shoulder line and restrained pendant make this read as a monarch without buying medieval armor.

The crown and mantle are the two useful places to spend. Avoid padding the outfit with many accessories that disappear under low room light.

## Three wearable modes
| Mode | Appearance | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Host | Antlers, mantle, face clear; veil clipped back | Greeting, drinks, moving around |
| Oracle king | Veil lowered briefly, hands free | Photo or 60–90-second story moment |
| Dance | Veil off; mantle removed if warm | Comfortable movement; crown stays only if secure |

## Build sequence
1. Fit the base outfit first; do a squat/sit/stairs test and check pockets.
2. Choose a broad headband or lightweight cap base that can carry soft faux antlers. Try on before adding fabric; do not use real wood or heavy resin horns on the head.
3. Shape the silhouette upward rather than far sideways. Final width depends on doors and the host’s comfort, so do not order oversized antlers without measurements.
4. Attach the veil to the crown or a separate removable clip system. It must lift away from eyes and mouth easily. Open-knit texture gives the reference without needing heavy fur or a full mask.
5. Add a short shoulder mantle and cord belt. Secure loose fabric and make the mantle removable quickly.
6. Add one small bone-like charm and one oxblood detail. The three raven skulls already in the decor cart have scenery allocations; do not double-count them as costume supplies without reassigning one.
7. Rehearse 30–45 minutes while talking, sitting and moving. Test with the actual room lighting before finalizing.

## Alternative palettes
- **Ivory and bone:** closest to Antler Queen; strong against the dark bedroom.
- **Charcoal and old gold:** a darker ceremonial king; use the Green Knight halo mood as a secondary reference, not a second heavy crown.
- **Ivory with moss/ochre:** softer woodland ruler; bring autumn in through one shoulder ornament.

These are design allowances, not researched price quotes. Working cap $150; choose reuse or simplify to stay under it. A separate winner crown should be budgeted to the story props, not silently added here.

Completion: the costume looks intentional with the veil up, is comfortable for hosting, and does not need a helper every time it is removed.
