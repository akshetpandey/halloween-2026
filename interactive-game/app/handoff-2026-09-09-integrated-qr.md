## Result

The sealed entrance now serves `src/client/assets/partiful-qr-art.webp`, a 1280px woodland illustration generated by the signed-in Oysiyl artistic QR Space. Trees, doors and mist form the QR structure. No QR overlay or raster repair is added by the app. [Exact prompt/settings](src/client/assets/partiful-qr-art.settings.json). The selected render uses first/final ControlNet strengths 0.7/0.9 and seed 718313. A preceding 1.0/1.0 variant also passed native checks, but the selected version offers more visual detail and purple light.

Only the normalized exact Partiful payload selects this bitmap. Personalized invitation codes and query-bearing/unrelated URLs retain their own dynamic code. The existing small invitation link stays below the image. The site and artwork are hosted on Cloudflare; the image generator is not called at runtime. No guest data, invitation tokens or credentials were submitted to the generator. No shortener, account creation or purchase occurred.
