## How your history filtered the pool

All **1,608 existing review records across both rooms + 140 old bedroom-pool records** were excluded by URI, normalized ISRC or shared-artist base title. That rejects previously reviewed alternate releases/remixes too. Peach, Therapy, Wait For It, E Samba, Open Air and Something Good Can Work were among the source leads excluded by history. A Cut is not an artist-wide ban: a specific existing Keep must still supply the proposed connection.

Known lonely/heartbreak or mixed-negative narratives were deferred, including Xtatic Truth and Always More. Acid-led, lengthy and source-intro leads were also deferred. Source inclusion by a DJ does not automatically establish room fit. No popularity or artist-wide approval shortcut.

Each track card gives its published source, **closest specific Keep**, an editorial comparison, the chosen version and vocal uncertainty. Those comparisons are hypotheses from attributable sources/catalog context, **not a claim of full audio listening**. Longer originals need to earn their time.
