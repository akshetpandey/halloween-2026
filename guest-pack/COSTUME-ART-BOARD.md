# Come as something the forest remembers
Eight artworks to borrow from: a shape, a color, a texture, an attitude. Any look can be worn by anyone. You can invent your own entity; one striking detail over clothes you already own is enough.

These boards use existing human-made artworks sourced online. The images are arranged and captioned without generating new figures or altering the artworks.

## A couture direction, if you want to go further
- [Alexander McQueen, Widows of Culloden, 2006–07 — The Met](https://blog.metmuseum.org/alexandermcqueen/dress-widows-of-culloden/): antlers emerging through lace. Borrow the contrast between something wild and something delicate; a small headpiece and secondhand lace are enough. Photograph credited by the museum to Sølve Sundsbø / Art + Commerce.
- [Iris van Herpen, Roots of Rebirth, 2021 — designer collection](https://www.irisvanherpen.com/collections/roots-of-rebirth): fungi-inspired couture. Borrow branching lines, pleats and translucent layers as a collar, sleeve or makeup pattern.

These fashion links open the original galleries. The shareable boards above contain the eight public-domain art references, with credits retained.

## An easy way to choose
Pick one base color: moss, bark, oxblood, ivory, smoke or tarnished gold. Add one symbol: a leaf, fruit, crescent, horn, wing pattern or mushroom shape. Choose shoes and layers you can dance in. Keep wings and antlers compact for the apartment.
