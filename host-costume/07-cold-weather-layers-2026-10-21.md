### Wearing and packing

- Keep the gold jewelry supported by the base outfit/body, **not attached through cardigan/coat**, so outer layers come off independently. Check smooth lapels and knit for chain snagging. No tight throat fastening.
- Put coat/cardigan on before replacing crown; do not force pullovers over the halo or bone tines. Keep cuffs accessible rather than tight over thick sleeves.
- Close the coat and cover ears/hands when cold; do not keep it open merely to show the chain. Decorative warmth is not the priority on a dawn trip home.
- Warm socks in the owned shoes; optional thin thermal bottoms only if jeans allow comfortable movement and indoor heat is manageable. No extra shoes assumed or sourced.
- Take off layers before overheating indoors and put them back on before going out; don't let a damp top be the only garment for the return journey.
- Use venue coat check for the substantial coat when appropriate; keep a manageable removable midlayer available if exploring outdoor stages. Exact re-access arrangements/queues unverified. Secure phone in a monitored zipped pocket, not a loose open tote.
- Test costume plus layers sitting, reaching and turning for at least 15–20 minutes; then do the existing longer costume rehearsal if time allows. Check coat hem/stairs, chain catching, pockets, warmth, quick removal and crown clearance.
