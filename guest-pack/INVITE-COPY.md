# The Hollow Court
*Come as something the forest remembers.*

For one night, the apartment belongs to the old gods.

Join us this Halloween for a moonlit woodland gathering: autumn leaves, strange spirits, deep-red cocktails, and a darker dance floor beneath the roots.

Come as a pagan god or goddess, forest spirit, dryad, faun, witch, mushroom creature, harvest deity, or anything that might wander out of an enchanted October wood. DIY and thrifted looks are very welcome: a leaf crown and a little imagination count.

There will be places to talk, things to watch, and room to dance. A few small mysteries may find you along the way.
