## Bulb and what “synced” means
For this room, assume coordinated **Dusk / October Grove / Normal** scenes and group brightness control. Music beat synchronization and matching the TV frame-by-frame are separate capabilities and are not required by this plan.

LIFX supports group controls and saved scenes. A scene can give each lamp a different complementary color/brightness while recalling the whole arrangement together. Its support guidance says scenes/schedules require connection to the LIFX Cloud account. This is not a promise of perfectly simultaneous transitions or identical colors across different bulb generations and shades.

- [LIFX groups](https://support.lifx.com/hc/en-us/articles/14508998551703-Controlling-your-lights-at-location-groups-and-individual-levels).
- [LIFX scenes](https://support.lifx.com/hc/en-us/articles/14509028240279-Scenes-in-the-LIFX-App).
- [LIFX integrations](https://www.lifx.com/pages/integrations).

Do not assume the owned bulb supports full color, very warm white or Matter until its model is identified. Older LIFX bulbs do not acquire every new model’s capability. Mixed WiZ/Hue/LIFX control through Google Home is another possible route, but supported shared commands and scene behavior need testing; vendor effects do not automatically transfer between brands.

[Current LIFX A19 product family](https://www.lifx.com/collections/color-a19) is the starting sourcing route. Set a provisional **$25–$40** bulb allowance; this is not a quote for a selected model. Prefer an E26 color bulb whose actual wattage is within the fixture rating, whose shape fits, and whose capabilities match the intended scenes. Smart bulbs stay continuously powered for app control; a physical on/off switch can cut them off. Avoid a fixture’s mains dimmer unless explicitly compatible.
